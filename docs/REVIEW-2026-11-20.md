# Schematic review, 2026-11-20

An independent review of the whole card against datasheets and published,
working designs: the Icepi Zero, the Raspberry Pi RP2040 design guide and Pico,
the PCM5102A and ECP5 datasheets, and the HuCARD-repro card. The netlist is clean:
no single-pin nets, no nets without a driver, no outputs shorted together, no
orphan parts.

## Found and fixed

| | problem | fix |
|---|---|---|
| **blocker** | **The edge fingers were mirrored.** HuCARD-repro numbers its pads from the other end (its pad k is console pin 39 − k). Our footprint took its pad numbers as console pins, so in the slot the console's +5 V landed on a ground finger. | `edge_footprint()` now has pin 38 at the left (front view, fingers down) and pin 1 at the right. Finger lengths are copied from the repro too: they stop 1.3 mm short of the edge, and the ground finger (pin 18) reaches 0.5 mm further so it mates first. The translator row is mirrored to sit over its fingers again. `fabcheck_card.py` now checks the order, so `fab_card.py` refuses a mirrored edge |
| minor | R101 (/OE) and R103 (/IRQ2 input) pull-ups at 10k lose to the ECP5's pull-down during configuration (up to 150 µA, ECP5 datasheet §4.1): 1.8 V, under V_IH | 3.3k (2.8 V) |
| minor | PCM5102A: one shared 10 µF for three supplies; datasheet Fig. 33 shows 0.1 + 10 µF on each | C150 and C151 (10 µF) added on CPVDD and AVDD |
| note | CO_IRQ (FPGA to RP2040) reads as an interrupt while the FPGA is unconfigured | R136, 10k pull-up |
| note | CARD.md had wrong counts and banks | corrected |

## Checked and correct

- **HuCard pinout:** all 38 pins agree with the wiki, the console schematic (CN202) and the repro. Pin 18 is the only ground; pin 1 /CD is grounded as a card-present signal.
- **Translators:** directions, enables and supply sides are right; the 33 Ω resistors are on the console side.
- **Power:** the diodes stop back-feeding between the console and both USB ports. The regulator dividers give 1.10, 2.51 and 3.33 V.
- **ECP5:** the core is identical to the Icepi. CFG is master SPI; PROGRAMN, INITN and DONE are pulled up; the clock is on a clock-capable ball.
- **RP2040:** decoupling, 27 Ω on USB, BOOTSEL, RUN and the crystal circuit follow the Raspberry Pi minimal design.
- **USB-C:** a separate 5.1k on each CC pin; both D+/D− rows connected; ESD on the connector side.
- **PCM5102A:** strap pins, charge-pump caps and the output filter are right.
- **Footprints:** crystal, LED, diode, SOT-23-5 and TSSOP pinouts are right.

## Owner decisions (2026-11-20)

- **Jack:** line out, as designed. No headphone amplifier.
- **Polyfuse:** add one now, between the slot's 5 V finger (pin 38) and everything on the card,
  so the translators' 5 V side is covered as well. Spec: hold 750 mA, trip 1.5 A, rated 6 V or
  more, 0805 or 1206, R below 0.3 ohm. Reasons: the card is estimated at 150-300 mA, and a
  polyfuse's hold current falls to about 70 % when warm inside the console, so 500 mA would be
  too close. The drop is below 0.1 V at 300 mA; with the Schottky, the rail stays about 4.5 V.
  It goes in after the current route finishes: finger 38 becomes net +5V_SLOT, F101 joins it
  to +5V_CON, and only that short piece is re-routed.
